Hi there!

I'm a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Center of Mathematical Sciences and Applications and am also affiliated with the Department of Economics at Harvard University.

My research is in econometrics, with a focus on identification problems for credible causal inference and nonparametric methods for policy evaluation. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Cornell University in 2026 and will join the Department of Economics at the University of Maryland as an Assistant Professor in Fall 2027.

I co-organize a seminar series on econometrics, statistics, and related fields at Harvard CMSA. Anyone interested is welcome to attend!
